Child Safety Standards - MiniJem

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

MiniJem ("the App," "we," "us," or "our"), developed by Joseph Teoh ("Developer"), is committed to maintaining a safe community environment. We strictly prohibit any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) across all Voices, audio replies, cover photos, profiles, and interactions on our platform.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy on CSAE and CSAM

MiniJem enforces an absolute zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Specifically, we prohibit:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, or soliciting any audio recording ("Voice"), image, text, or link that depicts, promotes, or facilitates child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Grooming, sexualizing minors, or engaging in predatory behavior toward anyone under the age of 18.
  • Using the platform to distribute or link to external CSAM or exploitative material.

2. Age Eligibility and Safeguards

MiniJem is intended strictly for users aged 13 and older (or the minimum age required by local law). We require date of birth verification during account setup to restrict underage access and enforce age-appropriate content boundaries.

3. In-App User Reporting

Users can report concerning Voices, audio comments, or user profiles directly inside the MiniJem app using our built-in reporting tool (under categories including Sexual Content, Abuse, and Illegal Acts). Users and members of the public can also report urgent child safety concerns via email at contact.ddmhq@gmail.com.

4. Moderation, Account Termination, and Legal Reporting

We review all child safety reports with highest priority. If we identify any content or account violating our child safety standards:

  • The offending Voice, photo, or profile is immediately removed from MiniJem.
  • The responsible user account is permanently banned from the platform.
  • Confirmed instances of CSAM or CSAE are reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant regional and national law enforcement authorities in accordance with applicable laws.

5. Designated Child Safety Point of Contact

For questions regarding MiniJem's child safety practices, CSAM prevention compliance, or law enforcement inquiries, please contact our designated safety point of contact:

Email: contact.ddmhq@gmail.com
