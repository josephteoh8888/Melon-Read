Delete Your MiniJem Account

App: MiniJem
Developer: Joseph Teoh

This page explains how to permanently delete your MiniJem account and associated data.

1. Delete Account Inside the App (Recommended)

If you still have MiniJem installed:

  1. Open the MiniJem app.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and associated data will be permanently deleted from our servers.

2. Request Deletion by Email (If You Uninstalled the App)

If you no longer have the app installed and wish to delete your account and associated data, send an email to contact.ddmhq@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request from the Google account email address linked to your MiniJem account.

Upon receipt, your account and associated user data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

3. What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted (via the in-app option or email request), the following data is permanently removed:

  • User profile information
  • Posts
  • Audio recordings
  • Comments
  • Any other personal data associated with the account

We retain only what is required by law (if any) and anonymized analytics that cannot identify you.

Note: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. After the process is complete you will no longer be able to sign in with that account.
